# Engineering Blogs and Production Stories

Real-world engineering posts reveal constraints and failure modes that simplified diagrams hide. Treat each post as a case study: what was the workload, what failed, what changed, and what would not generalize?

## Suggested engineering sources

- [Netflix Technology Blog](https://netflixtechblog.com/) — streaming, resilience, observability and platform engineering.
- [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/) — Internet infrastructure, networking, edge systems and incident analysis.
- [Uber Engineering](https://www.uber.com/blog/engineering/) — marketplace, geospatial, data and platform systems.
- [Discord Engineering](https://discord.com/blog) — chat scale, storage, infrastructure and reliability.
- [Meta Engineering](https://engineering.fb.com/) — feed, storage, infrastructure and AI systems.
- [Google Research](https://research.google/) — research context and selected system papers.
- [Stripe Engineering](https://stripe.com/blog/engineering) — payments, APIs, reliability and developer infrastructure.
- [GitHub Engineering](https://github.blog/engineering/) — developer platform, databases and distributed services.
- [Amazon Builders' Library](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/) — operational lessons and distributed-system design practices.
- [Cloudflare outage analysis](https://blog.cloudflare.com/tag/outage/) — failure analysis from a large edge network.

## Reading worksheet

For every post, answer:
1. What system and user workload are described?
2. What constraint or failure triggered the work?
3. What measurements were used?
4. What was the old architecture and why did it stop working?
5. What changed, and what new trade-offs appeared?
6. Which parts generalize, and which depend on that company's context?
