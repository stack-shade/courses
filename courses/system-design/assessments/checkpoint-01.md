# Checkpoint 01 · Foundations

Try to answer these without looking at the lessons. Write your reasoning, not just a term.

1. A product prompt says "build a scalable photo app". What five clarifying questions would you ask?
2. Define one functional requirement and three measurable NFRs for photo uploads.
3. Convert 25 million requests/day into average requests/second.
4. If the peak-to-average multiplier is 8×, what peak request rate does that imply?
5. Why can average latency look healthy while a portion of users experience very slow requests?
6. Draw a browser-to-database request path. Mark at least four places where latency or failure may occur.
7. What is one reason a CDN may fail to reduce origin load?
8. Give one benefit and one cost of caching.
9. What should be measured before deciding to shard a database?
10. Describe one way to make analytics processing asynchronous without losing track of failed events.

## Self-assessment

For each answer, score 0–3 using the [readiness rubric](../roadmap.md#readiness-rubric). Rewrite any answer scored 0 or 1 after revisiting the relevant lesson.
